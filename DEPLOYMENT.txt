JAZZY BOT AI Vercel deployment

1. Deploy the folder/repository.
2. Add DERIV_CLIENT_ID, DERIV_REDIRECT_URI and DERIV_SCOPE=trade in Vercel Environment Variables.
3. Register the exact HTTPS DERIV_REDIRECT_URI in your Deriv OAuth app.
4. Redeploy.
5. Open the production URL and press Real -> Connect Deriv.

Never commit secrets or send them in chat.
